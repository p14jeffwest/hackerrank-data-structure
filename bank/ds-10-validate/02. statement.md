## Validating a BST

A binary tree is a **binary search tree** when, at **every** node, all keys in
its left subtree are smaller and all keys in its right subtree are larger.

Decide whether each given tree is one.

```
   10
   / \
  5   25
     /  \
    2    45
```

This is not a BST. `2` is a legal left child of `25`, but it lies in the right
subtree of `10` and is smaller than `10`. **A node is constrained by every
ancestor, not just its parent.**

Keys are distinct in a valid tree, and may be any `int` -- including
`Integer.MIN_VALUE` and `Integer.MAX_VALUE`.

Fill in:

```java
static boolean isValidBST(Node root)
```
