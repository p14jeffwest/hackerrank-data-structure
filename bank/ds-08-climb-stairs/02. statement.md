## Climbing Stairs

You are climbing a staircase, taking either **1 or 2 steps** at a time.

Starting from the ground, count the **distinct ways** to land exactly on step
`N`.

### Example

For `N = 5` the answer is `8`:

```
1+1+1+1+1    1+1+1+2    1+1+2+1    1+2+1+1
2+1+1+1      1+2+2      2+1+2      2+2+1
```

Two routes that take the same steps in a different order count separately.

`N` reaches 90, and the count grows quickly. **Work out how large the answer
gets before choosing the type you store it in** -- an overflow here throws
nothing and warns about nothing.
