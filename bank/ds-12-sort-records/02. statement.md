## Ranking by Score

Participants are given with their names and scores, **in the order they signed
up**. Order them from the highest score down.

**Participants on the same score keep the order they signed up in.** The one
who signed up earlier comes first.

### Example

| Sign-up | Name | Score |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `sora` | 90 |
| 2 | `junho` | 75 |
| 3 | `mina` | 90 |
| 4 | `daeun` | 82 |
| 5 | `yuna` | 75 |

```
sora 90
mina 90
daeun 82
junho 75
yuna 75
```

`sora` and `mina` both scored 90, and `sora` signed up first. The alphabetical
order of the names has nothing to do with it.

```java
static Student[] sortByScore(Student[] students)
```

`Student` holds a `name` and a `score` and is already defined.
