## The Median of a Data Stream

Numbers arrive one at a time. At any moment you may be asked for the **median**
of everything received so far -- the middle value, or the average of the two
middle values when the count is even.

```
add 1  add 2   ->  median = 1.5
add 3          ->  median = 2.0
```

Complete two methods of `MedianFinder`:

```java
public void addNum(int x)      // must be O(log n)
public double findMedian()     // must be O(1)
```

Two heaps are provided: `lower` holds the smaller half with its **largest** on
top, and `upper` holds the larger half with its **smallest** on top. Keep them
that way -- every value in `lower` at most every value in `upper`, and their
sizes never differing by more than one -- and the median is always at one of
the two tops.

Sorting the numbers on each request is far too slow here.
