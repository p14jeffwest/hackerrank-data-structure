## The Tower of Hanoi

There are three pegs, called **A**, **B** and **C**.

At the start, peg A holds `N` disks of different sizes, **largest at the
bottom, smallest at the top**. The goal is to move all of them to peg **C**.

Two rules:

- Only one disk may be moved at a time.
- **A larger disk may never rest on a smaller one.**

Peg B is available as a spare.

Print the **minimum number of moves** needed, and then the moves themselves.

### Example

Three disks take seven moves:

```
A -> C
A -> B
C -> B
A -> C
B -> A
B -> C
A -> C
```

The problem is smaller than it looks. To move `N` disks to C, first get the
top `N-1` out of the way, move the largest one, and then bring the `N-1` back
on top of it. Both of those are the same problem with one disk fewer -- what
changes is which peg is playing which role.
