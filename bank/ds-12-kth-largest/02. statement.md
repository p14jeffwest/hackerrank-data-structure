## The K-th Largest Value

Given `N` integers, report the **`k`-th largest**. `k` starts at 1, so `k = 1`
is the maximum.

**Equal values are counted separately.** In `9 9 9 5 1` the 2nd largest is
`9` and the 4th is `5`.

| Values | `k` | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| `3 2 1 5 6 4` | 2 | `5` |
| `3 2 3 1 2 4 5 5 6` | 4 | `4` |

```java
static int kthLargest(int[] nums, int k)
```

Taking the maximum out `k` times costs $O(N \cdot k)$ and will not finish
here.
