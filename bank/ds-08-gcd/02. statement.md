## Greatest Common Divisor

The **greatest common divisor** of two positive integers is the largest number
that divides both of them.

The greatest common divisor of `48` and `36` is `12`. For `17` and `5` it is
`1`, since they share no factor above 1.

Use this property:

> The greatest common divisor of `a` and `b` is the same as the greatest
> common divisor of `b` and the remainder of `a` divided by `b`.

Complete the `gcd` method using **recursion**:

```java
static int gcd(int a, int b)
```

All input and output handling is provided.
