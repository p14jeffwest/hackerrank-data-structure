## Merge Intervals

Given intervals `[start, end]`, replace every group of overlapping intervals
with the single interval covering it, and report the result in ascending order
of start.

```
[1,3] [2,6] [8,10] [15,18]  ->  [1,6] [8,10] [15,18]
[1,4] [4,5]                 ->  [1,5]
```

**Intervals that merely touch overlap here.** `[1,4]` and `[4,5]` become
`[1,5]`.

```java
static int[][] merge(int[][] intervals)
```

`intervals[i]` is `{start, end}`, in no particular order. You may rearrange
the array.

Comparing every pair is $O(n^2)$ and will not finish. Putting the intervals in
the right order first makes one pass enough.
