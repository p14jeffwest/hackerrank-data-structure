Print one line for each `search`, `print` and `preorder` command.

`print` lists the keys in ascending order, separated by single spaces;
`preorder` lists them in preorder. An empty tree prints an empty line.

### Example

Input

```
9
insert 50
insert 30
insert 70
insert 20
insert 45
preorder
delete 50
preorder
print
```

Output

```
50 30 20 45 70
45 30 20 70
20 30 45 70
```

Removing the root `50` promotes `45`, the largest key in its left subtree.
