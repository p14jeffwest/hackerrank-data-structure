## Daily Temperatures

You are given the temperature recorded on each of `n` consecutive days. For
every day, report **how many days later** the first warmer day arrives. If no
warmer day ever comes, report `0`.

```
73 74 75 71 69 72 76 73
 1  1  4  2  1  1  0  0
```

Day 0 is followed immediately by a warmer day, so its answer is 1. Day 2
records 75 and has to wait until day 6, four days later. The last two days
never see anything warmer.

A day of the **same** temperature is not warmer.

Answering each day by scanning forward from it is easy to write and far too
slow at this size. Something has to remember the days that are still waiting.
