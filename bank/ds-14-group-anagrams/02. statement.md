## Grouping Anagrams

Two words are **anagrams** when one is a rearrangement of the other -- the
same letters in the same quantities. Group the given words so that anagrams
are together.

```
eat tea tan ate nat bat

->  eat tea ate
    tan nat
    bat
```

### The order is part of the answer

- Within a group, the words keep the order they had in the input.
- The groups come in the order their **first** word appeared in the input.

So for `zzz abc cba yyy zzz bac`, the first group is `zzz zzz`.

```java
static List<List<String>> groupAnagrams(String[] strs)
```

A hash map makes the grouping a lookup rather than a search -- but a plain
`HashMap` has no order of its own.
