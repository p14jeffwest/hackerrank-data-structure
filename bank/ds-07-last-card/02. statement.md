## The Last Remaining Card

A deck of `N` cards lies in a row, each with a number written on it. Repeat the
following until **one card remains**:

1. **Discard** the card at the front.
2. **Move** the next card to the back of the deck.

Print the discarded cards in the order they were discarded, and then the card
that remains.

### Example

The deck `10 20 30 40`:

| Step | Deck | Discarded |
|---|---|---|
| start | `10 20 30 40` | |
| 1 | `30 40 20` | `10` |
| 2 | `20 40` | `30` |
| 3 | `40` | `20` |

So the discard order is `10 30 20` and the last card is `40`.

Both steps take from the front, and one of them puts a card back at the other
end. Note that `N` reaches 500,000: a structure whose front removal shifts
everything behind it will not finish in time.
